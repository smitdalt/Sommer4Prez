Why Vote for Us?

We decided to put our campaigns together as we believe strong leadership means representing every voice! Together, we bring diverse perspectives, proven leadership experience, and established relationships with faculty to make communication clear and effective. Our goal is to foster an inclusive, supportive environment where every student feels heard, while working side-by-side to strengthen our community and ensure our first year is the best it can be. We have worked together in many capacities for over a year now and have proven to complement each other well!
